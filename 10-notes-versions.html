Version history

Six mega releases in four days. Here is what each one added.

v3.99Multiverse ModeLatestNov 16, 2026
  • Chat Games - Tic Tac Toe, full-rules Chess (castling, en passant, promotion) and Doodle Battle with voting, live inside chat.
  • Collab Notes - friends edit the same note at the same time, changes appear live.
  • Shared Lists - grocery, trip or work lists where every tick shows up live for everyone.
  • Voice messages - record, send and play without leaving the chat.
  • Shared Village - grow one Hidden Leaf Village together, every contribution counted.
  • Friend Ranks - an XP leaderboard for just the friends you chat with.
  • Full row-level security on the server: only the people in a game, note or list can see its data.
v3.98Instant Chat + PacksNov 15, 2026
  • Instant Chat - private chat with only a username and password. No phone, no email, no personal data.
  • Text, photos and files up to 25 MB, with sent / delivered / read ticks, unread badges, search and delete for everyone.
  • Packs - 3 original sound packs, 6 open-source fonts, 24 original stickers and the Leaf Catch mini game.
  • The internet is used only by the chat screen; the rest of the app stays fully offline.
v3.97Sage ModeNov 15, 2026
  • Design system - one set of spacing, type and depth rules applied to every page.
  • Motion engine - press feedback and smooth transitions, with a Reduced mode that follows your phone settings.
  • Tools page redesign - search, category chips and a Recent list.
  • First impression - new original icon, animated splash and a 4-slide welcome tour.
  • Skeleton loaders, illustrated empty states and haptics on taps, switches and sliders.
v3.96Baryon ModeNov 15, 2026
  • Tool Maker - build your own no-code tools from buttons, counters, checklists, notes and timers.
  • Theme Studio - recolor the whole app, with presets and shareable theme codes.
  • Hidden Leaf Village - your finished tasks, notes and habits grow a living village.
  • Sensei 2.0 - chain commands like "torch on + off in 10 minutes" and teach your own shortcuts.
  • QR Bridge - send notes, tools and themes to another phone offline, through QR frames.
  • Home Command Center - a home-screen widget with Sensei, Daily Scroll and your favorite actions.
v3.95Hokage ModeNov 15, 2026
  • Sensei - an offline Hinglish command bar, typed or spoken: alarms, vault, notes, math and more.
  • Jutsu Macros - 20 presets and your own builder: shake, wave, back tap, charger and time triggers.
  • Daily Scroll - one screen with today’s tasks, reminders, calendar, habits and a one-line plan.
  • Mission + Rank - climb from Academy Student to Hokage with XP, daily missions and badges.
  • Shadow Clone - home-screen shortcuts and a 5-button widget.
  • Seal Search - fuzzy search across notes, tasks, tools, settings and the vault.
v3.94Professional InterfacesNov 12, 2026
  • 10 professional layouts for Home, notes, tasks, reminders and archive: Bento Studio, Editorial Paper, Swiss Grid, Command Center, Blueprint Pro, Ledger Desk, Atelier Canvas, Ribbon Flow, Gallery Folio and Compact Pro.
  • Live previews, a NEW badge and one-tap restore to the original look.
  • Layouts work independently of your color and festival themes.
