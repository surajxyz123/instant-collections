Straight talk

Good to know

  • Instant Notes is not on the Play Store. You install it directly (sideload) - it takes under a minute and your data stays on update.
  • Chat and Multiverse features run on a small free server. It has free-tier limits and pauses after a week of no use; one tap brings it back.
  • Multiverse features need your friends on v3.99 too - send them the install page.
  • Chats are not end-to-end encrypted. Keep passwords and very private things out of chat.
  • No push notifications: new messages appear while the app is open.
