Honest notes

Before you install

  • Instant Notes is not on the Play Store. You install it directly, which Android calls sideloading.
  • Keep Google Play Protect on. If it shows a warning, that is normal for apps installed outside the Play Store - you can choose "Install anyway", or simply skip installing.
  • Chat, games and other Multiverse features run on a small free server: files up to 25 MB, and the server may pause after a week of nobody using it (one tap brings it back).
  • To play games, co-edit notes or share lists with a friend, both of you need v3.99 - send them this page.
  • Chats are not end-to-end encrypted, so keep passwords and very private things out of chat. There are no push notifications; new messages appear while the app is open.

Why it is safe to try

  • No ads, no trackers, no account needed for the app itself.
  • Chat signup asks only for a username and password - no phone number, no email.
  • Everything except chat works fully offline, on your own phone.
